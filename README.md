# 👋 Hi, I'm Pleniluner

> **Architecture × Computing × Radio × Arts**

Greetings to friends from all corners of the world.
**Pax omnibus. — Peace to all.**

I am an architecture student exploring the intersections between **built form, computation, signal systems, and artistic expression**.

My work and studies currently revolve around architectural design and BIM, computer science and Android development, amateur radio and embedded systems, and the relationship between traditional Chinese aesthetics and contemporary design.

---

## 🧭 About Me

🏗️ **Architecture & BIM**
Architecture is my primary discipline. I focus on conceptual design, spatial composition, architectural visualization, BIM workflows, and the exploration of a contemporary **Neo-Chinese architectural language**.

💻 **Computer Science & Android**
I study algorithms, software engineering, machine learning, computer vision, embedded systems, and mobile development. My current Android stack centers on **Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and the Android SDK**.

📡 **Amateur Radio & SDR**
Licensed Class A amateur radio operator **BG7LBJ**. Currently active on VHF/UHF with a modified **Quansheng UV-K5**, while developing my understanding of RF fundamentals, SDR, DSP, digital communications, and signal analysis.

🖌️ **Art & Built Form**
Architectural sketching and Chinese ink wash painting shape the way I think about **mass, void, rhythm, restraint, and atmosphere**. I am especially interested in translating these principles into contemporary architecture.

🎸 **Music & Strategy**
Learning acoustic guitar on a **Yamaha FG800M** and enjoying Xiangqi, Shogi, and International Chess. To me, both music and strategy share something with architecture: structure, tension, rhythm, and composition.

🌌 **Astronomy & Exploration**
The night sky remains one of my strongest sources of curiosity. I am interested in observational astronomy, computational imaging, celestial-event prediction, and the use of software to understand physical systems.

## 🔬 Current Deep Dives

### 📡 SDR, DSP & RF

Studying software-defined radio, digital signal processing, spectrum analysis, modulation, signal decoding, antenna behavior, and practical VHF/UHF operating techniques.

My current radio platform is the **Quansheng UV-K5**, running modified firmware with spectrum visualization and expanded receive capabilities.

### 📱 Modern Android Development

Exploring:

* Jetpack Compose
* advanced UI animation
* gesture-driven interfaces
* application architecture
* performance optimization
* real-time communication
* device-specific Android behavior

### 🏯 Architectural Theory

I am investigating how principles found in traditional Chinese visual culture can inform contemporary architecture.

Chinese ink wash painting is particularly important to this exploration: its treatment of **emptiness and substance, density and silence, restraint and suggestion** offers a useful conceptual vocabulary for spatial design.

### 🧮 Mathematics & Algorithms

My mathematical interests include:

* algebra
* geometry
* number theory
* discrete mathematics
* group theory
* mathematical analysis

I use mathematics both as a theoretical foundation and as a tool for algorithmic problem solving.
